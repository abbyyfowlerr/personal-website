A little about me

I grew up in Delaware and now live in the DC area. I studied neuroscience and computer science at the University of Delaware and currently work on medical imaging research at the NIH.

I'm interested in the ways technology and medicine can work together, but there's plenty I enjoy outside of work, too. I like running, reading, baking, and finding new places to explore.

This site is an informal collection of the things that make up my days, rather than a complete account of everything I've done.
